
# SR-22 — Documented and Rehearsed Incident-Response Plan

## Requirement
The Vote Counting and Tabulation subsystem shall maintain a documented incident-response plan
that defines how security incidents are identified, reported, assessed, contained, investigated, recovered from and reviewed.

The plan should assign responsibilities, define escalation procedures and establish communication 
and recovery processes to support an organised response to security incidents.

## Owner
Rosie

## Status
Analysis only — not implemented in the prototype.

## Security objective
Minimise the impact of security incidents on the confidentiality, integrity and availability
of the Vote Counting and Tabulation subsystem. The plan should help the team respond consistently,
preserve relevant evidence and restore services safely without compromising the integrity of election results.

## Threats
- Delayed detection or reporting of security incidents.
- Unclear responsibilities during an incident.
- Inconsistent containment and recovery decisions.
- Loss or alteration of evidence needed for investigation.
- Premature restoration of a compromised tallying service.
